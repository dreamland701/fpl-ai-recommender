# G5: the RL decision layer, rebuilt (October 2026)

*Approved by Wes on 2026-10-07: "the RL will need env rebuild ... pls proceed when ready / GPU has cycles."*

## Why rebuild

The review (`Codex/CODE_FINDINGS.md`) found that the G2/G3 environment's evidence could not be trusted.

| Finding | G2/G3 behaviour | G5 |
|---|---|---|
| P0-04: future information | The opening prompt's week-by-week outlook was built from each *later* week's own forecasts. | The outlook comes only from the origin week's 1/3/5-week heads. A test mutates later weeks and requires an identical prompt. |
| P1-07: action space | At most one transfer per week, chosen from the heuristic's bottom-6 search; no Wildcard or Free Hit. | Each week offers hold, best single transfer, or best two transfers, all generated by the exact optimizer (`fpl_ai/squad_milp.py`) for the squad the plan has actually reached. All four chips are available. |
| P1-08: unfair baseline | Rewards were measured against a planner that never played chips, so spending a chip looked like skill. | Rewards are measured against a deterministic planner holding the same chips and free transfers. It plays Triple Captain and Bench Boost in the window weeks with the highest origin-projected value, and never plays Wildcard or Free Hit; see [chip calibration](../reports/gw6_packet_2026_10/chips_gw7_memo.md). |
| P1-09: evaluation | The squad was reset between windows. | Closed-loop season evaluation (`scripts/run_grpo_plan_eval_v2.py`): each week the policy plans from the state it actually reached, only week 1 is executed, and the state carries forward. |
| P1-10: parsing | One object plus defaults counted as a valid plan. | Exactly K schema-valid actions or nothing. Invalid output scores −5 in training and falls back to the planner in evaluation, where it is counted. |
| Scoring | No autosubs; selling prices were never refreshed; free-transfer cap of 2. | Official rules (`fpl_ai/rules.py`): autosubs, vice-captain cover, half-profit selling, free-transfer cap by season (2 to 2023-24, 5 from 2024-25). |

## The task

Each week of a five-week plan is `{"transfer": "hold"|"best1"|"best2", "captain": id|null, "chip": "none"|"bench_boost"|"triple_captain"|"wildcard"|"free_hit"}`.

The optimizer turns `best1`/`best2`/`wildcard`/`free_hit` into concrete legal moves at execution time, using that week's forecasts. The policy chooses *when* and *what kind*; the optimizer chooses *who*. This is the part the review said should be learnable and checkable.

## Data and evaluation

- **States** (`scripts/build_grpo_state_cache_v2.py`): fixed-pipeline classic+F6 forecasts fit as-of each week, real outcomes, and starting squads from fixed-pipeline baseline trajectories.
- **Training:** 2022-23, 2023-24 and 2024-25 (GW2–38) plus 2025-26 GW2–26, giving 120 five-week episodes. Chip budgets are sampled per episode and half-season.
- **Holdout:** 2025-26 GW27–38, closed loop. Reference points on that holdout:
  - same-chips planner: **766**
  - classic with no chips: **735**

  The planner's 31-point lead is exactly its Triple Captain and Bench Boost. That is the chip value the old baseline credited to the policy.
